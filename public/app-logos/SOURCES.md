## Gameiras

The original brand is HTML/CSS, not an image. `GameirasWordmark.tsx` preserves the source's lowercase `gameiras`, Outfit at weight 800, letter-spacing -0.06em, green initial (#1f6f62), dark text (#1f3440), and coral dot (#ef7458). Its compact `g` plus dot is an adaptation for small app avatars, not an extracted favicon.
